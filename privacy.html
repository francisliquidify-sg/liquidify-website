Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

At Liquidify, your financial privacy and data security are our top priority. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal and financial information when you submit documents through our website.

1. Data Collection & Purpose

When you submit financial documents through our secure upload form, you provide:

  • Your WhatsApp contact number
  • Your business name
  • Financial documents (bank statements, credit reports, financial statements, Notice of Assessment)

Purpose: We collect this information solely to assess your eligibility for various financing options. We analyze your financial profile to determine which banks and financing routes may be suitable for your business needs.

2. Data Security & Protection

Your data is protected through multiple security layers:

  • HTTPS Encryption: All file uploads are transmitted over a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS) to prevent interception.
  • Verified Domain: Our domain is verified with Meta and Cloudflare for additional security assurance.
  • Email Delivery: Documents are sent to our secure business email (admin@liquidify.sg) and are not stored on public servers.
  • Two-Factor Authentication: Our business email is protected with two-factor authentication to restrict access.
  • Confidentiality: Only authorized Liquidify team members have access to your documents.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

We only retain your documents for as long as necessary to process your application.

Specifically:

  • Your documents are reviewed and assessed within 2-5 business days of submission.
  • Once we have provided you with financing recommendations and next steps, your documents are securely deleted.
  • If you do not proceed with our service, your documents are deleted within 30 days of submission.
  • We do not maintain long-term archives of financial documents without your explicit consent.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We will not share your financial information with third parties without your explicit consent.

The only exception is when necessary to:

  • Facilitate Your Application: With your permission, we may share relevant information with the specific bank or financial institution you choose to apply to.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by Singapore law, regulation, or court order.

We are a broker, not a lender. We do not sell or rent your data to marketing firms, advertisers, or other commercial entities.

5. Your Rights & Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request to see what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your documents before or after your application is processed.
  • Opt-Out: Stop communication with Liquidify at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@liquidify.sg or via WhatsApp at +65 8193 2026.

6. Compliance & Regulations

Liquidify operates as a licensed loan broker in Singapore and complies with:

  • Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA): Singapore's data protection law governing the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.
  • Meta Financial Services Policy: Transparency requirements for financial services advertising and data handling.
  • Banking Regulations: Safe handling of financial information according to Singapore banking standards.

Disclaimer: We are a financing broker, not a lender. We do not provide credit or make lending decisions — we connect you with financial institutions that assess your eligibility.

7. Updates to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our practices or regulations. We will notify you of any material changes via email or through updates on our website.
