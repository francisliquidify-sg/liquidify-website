Common questions

Before you message us

My business is new. Can I still qualify?

Yes. We work with startups through to established companies. Some lenders are far more open to newer businesses than others, and we know which.

What does it cost?

The assessment and planning are free. Our fee is agreed upfront and only payable once your financing is disbursed.

How fast can I get funded?

It depends on the lender and your documents. We tell you quickly whether your case is realistic, so you're not waiting weeks to hear a no.

I'm a foreigner who owns a business here. Can you help?

Often, yes. EP and Work Permit holders who own a Singapore business can be funded through private funders, mainly on the strength of the bank statements.
