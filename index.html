FAQ

Quick answers

What does it cost?

The assessment is free. Our fee is agreed upfront and only payable once your financing is disbursed.

Are you a bank or lender?

No. We're a financing broker. We match you with the right bank or funder, prepare the application and follow it through. The lending decision is always the lender's.

My business is new. Can I still qualify?

Yes. We work with startups through to established companies. Some lenders are far more open to newer businesses than others.

Will talking to you affect my credit?

No. Nothing touches your credit report until you approve a submission.
