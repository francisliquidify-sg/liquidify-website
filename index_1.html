Singapore SME business financing

There's more than one route to business financing.

Most business owners are assessed on company revenue alone — and stop asking when the answer is no. A second route exists, assessed on entirely different criteria. Knowing which one fits your business is the difference between a decline and a disbursement.

How your application gets assessed

Route A Assessed on company turnover

The traditional working capital loan. Banks look first at annual revenue, trading history and company creditworthiness. Businesses that fall short on those measures are declined on this route alone.

Route B Assessed on director's profile

An unsecured business facility weighted on the director's personal credit standing and income tax Notices of Assessment, rather than company turnover.

Two structures, two sets of eligibility criteria. A business that doesn't clear the first can still be viable on the second — which is the conversation most owners never get to have.

The part most owners miss

Turnover is a criterion, not a verdict.

Two facilities can fund the same business and be assessed on completely different things. Understanding which door you're knocking on changes the outcome — and, in one case, changes what the loan does to your borrowing capacity later.

Route A — the turnover test

Traditional working capital and term loans start with the company's financials. Revenue, trading history and company creditworthiness carry the assessment.

  • Best fit for established businesses with steady, documented revenue
  • Typically the lowest cost of funds when you qualify
  • Newer businesses, or those with thinner revenue, are frequently declined here

If this is a no, it is a no about this product — not about your business.

Route B — the director's profile

An unsecured business facility weighted on the director rather than the company. Personal credit standing and income tax Notices of Assessment do the heavy lifting.

  • Opens a route for newer or smaller-turnover businesses
  • Structured as a business facility, so it doesn't sit on your consumer credit file
  • Doesn't consume the TDSR headroom you'll need for a property purchase

Why that last point matters: if a mortgage is anywhere in your plans, how a facility is structured affects what you can borrow later.

Eligibility, structure and treatment vary by lender and by your circumstances. Nothing here is an offer of credit or a statement that you will qualify — the purpose of a consultation is to work out which route, if any, actually fits your numbers.

Questions owners actually ask

Answered plainly, before you get in touch.

My turnover is too low for a bank loan. Is it worth talking to you?

Very likely — that's precisely the situation where the assessment route matters. The traditional working capital loan leans on company revenue, but an unsecured facility assessed on the director's profile and income tax Notices of Assessment is a different test entirely. Whether you clear it depends on your personal credit standing and declared income, which is what a first conversation establishes.

What do you charge?

A service fee calculated as a percentage of the amount financed, agreed with you in writing before we submit anything, and payable on a successful outcome. The percentage depends on the complexity of the case and the facility involved. There is no charge for the initial conversation.

Was this useful?
Are you a lender?

No. Liquidify helps you find and apply for business financing. We assess your position, structure the application, and submit it through the relevant banker or financial institution. The lending decision, the rate and the terms are always the lender's.

Was this useful?
Will this affect my ability to buy property later?

It depends on how the facility is structured. A properly structured business facility is treated differently from personal borrowing when it comes to your consumer credit file and your TDSR headroom. If a property purchase is anywhere in your plans, say so at the outset — it should shape which route we pursue, not be discovered afterwards.

Was this useful?
What documents will I need?

Nothing to start the conversation. For an application, expect company registration details, recent bank statements, financial statements where available, and the director's income tax Notices of Assessment. We'll tell you exactly what's needed for your specific route before you gather anything.

Was this useful?
How long does it take?

It varies by lender and facility, and anyone quoting you a fixed timeline before seeing your case is guessing. What we can commit to is telling you quickly whether your case is realistic, so you're not waiting weeks to hear a no.

Was this useful?
What if I've been declined before?

A decline is specific to a lender, a product and a moment. It's worth understanding why — whether it was turnover, documentation, existing commitments or how the application was presented — because that determines whether a different route is viable or whether the honest answer is to strengthen your position first.
